# Third-party assets

## Stryker gurney

The Stryker gurney models and texture in `../ob_hospital_assets/stream/` come from
[`DonHulieo/don-emsprops`](https://github.com/DonHulieo/don-emsprops), a FiveM
conversion of Tiddy's Medical Prop Pack.

Included assets:

- `strykergurney.ydr` and `strykergurney.ytyp`
- `loweredstrykergurney.ydr` and `loweredstrykergurney.ytyp`
- `sittingstrykergurney.ydr` and `sittingstrykergurney.ytyp`
- `strykertexture.ytd`

Credits: DonHulieo for the FiveM conversion and Tiddy for the original models.
